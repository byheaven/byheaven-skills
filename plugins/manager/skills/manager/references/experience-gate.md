# Experience gates

Work that adds or changes what a person sees or operates, including the user's own tools such as a status line, pane, or other interface mod, passes both gates below before implementation or dispatch. A project's own design-engineering standard may refine the tiers and artifacts and may not weaken the invariants; these gates are the fallback when a project has none, and their invariants hold across projects. Source-local rules map storage and tooling and may strengthen the artifact or evidence.

## Review object gate

Whoever produces or reviews such a change applies the `experience-design` skill. For every such change, produce a real, durable, version-bound review object as soon as its direction is visible and before any implementation whose shape depends on that direction. Classify the delta once:

- interaction or mixed, heavy or medium: a clickable high-fidelity prototype with real copy, realistic data, and every applicable state, rendered at the reviewer's device viewport;
- game mechanic or 3D scene, heavy or medium: a playable graybox prototype on the target device for feel and pacing, a visual target (a concept image or one polished scene) for the look, and its frame rate measured on a real device;
- content-only: exact copy in its real UI context;
- visual-only: one representative high-fidelity sample;
- deterministic correction: the exact expected before/after result;
- light adjustment to an interaction or game feel: one rendered before/after of the adjusted element.

For a heavy or medium change, the review offers two or three versions drafted as [competing candidates](production.md#topology) wherever the outcome is open (flow, layout, mechanic, or look), each beside the benchmark's equivalent and carrying the scenarios from the experience bar below. Before any version reaches the owner, a fresh verifier renders it, compares it with the benchmark, and walks those scenarios; a version that fails is repaired or dropped before the review. The owner reviews no separate wireframe: when versions differ in flow, a one-page overview names the route each takes, and flow and look are decided in the same review.

Present that exact object under [Review presentation](production.md#review-presentation) before requesting judgment. In this interim direction review, the user or named product owner approves taste-bearing objects; a deterministic correction passes when focused evidence matches its exact contract. Experience approval settles the direction; an earlier effective Delivery request continues to cover implementation of the approved object while its Goal, Boundary, and Acceptance are unchanged, and without one the approval does not itself authorize implementation. Selection-independent service, data, contract, and test work may continue while the gate is open. Implementation scales only from the confirmed object, returns any missing or changed user-facing decision to the gate, and closes through evidence against that same object, a rendered comparison of the build for a heavy or medium change. Whoever produced it, Manager or a sub-agent, Manager acceptance closes one additional predicate for every user-visible delivery: the direction invariant below holds.

## Product decision gate

- **Heavy — adds or changes the structure of a user journey** (new feature, new loop, new page, cross-page flow): settle direction through [Grill](direction.md#grill) with the direction owner first; when the handoff leaves it unsettled, return it there under [Handoff](direction.md#handoff) (a refutable change is a value bet and enters as a five-part proposal), then present the candidates above across the complete journey, each carrying only its open decisions; sources and settled content are recorded in the task record or a repository artifact. Implementation is dispatched only after the owner has adjudicated every open decision. Economic values ship an editable numeric table.
- **Medium — a single-point change to an existing screen**: the candidates above for the changed screen, each shown before and after with its open decisions, adjudicated the same way before implementation.
- **Light — copy, defect fixes, adjustments that do not change structure**: owner review may be skipped only when no review predicate remains user-owned; otherwise it uses the content-only or deterministic-correction review object above.

Every review object that needs owner adjudication is presented under [Review presentation](production.md#review-presentation) before judgment is requested.

**Invariant (P1).** No product setting becomes settled content, or the basis for dispatched implementation, until the product owner has explicitly chosen it. An authorization to "write it up as a document" covers only recording what has been adjudicated; it does not promote producer proposals to settings. Likewise, a bare request to proceed ("just do it", "直接做") authorizes building but settles no setting that is otherwise open: choices the owner states in the request or the current context stay settled, the Light exception above still applies, and every other open setting reaches the owner in the review object before shape-dependent work, unless the owner explicitly waives that review. Inline annotations on the review object are the default adjudication channel; the remainder closes through structured questions.

**Invariant (direction).** The approved review object settles direction: the journey and its steps, states, actions, entry points, information priority, and what its copy says. Within that direction, implementation reaches the highest craft the work allows, beyond the object's fidelity: visual refinement, layout at every viewport, motion and feedback, and the states that follow from the approved ones by the product's conventions. A step, action, entry point, setting, or sentence the object does not contain, or a change in information priority, returns to this gate as one more frame for the owner to adjudicate, as does a missed state that carries a product choice.

**Experience bar.** A heavy or medium delivery carries in its Acceptance the benchmark the `experience-design` skill names and the two or three qualities of this job a leading team would refuse to ship without, each written as a scenario a person goes through, such as recovering a remembered moment or recognizing a received response as someone's gesture. Its judge is a fresh verifier that renders the candidate at the real viewports and states and walks those scenarios; fidelity to the review object, engineering tests, and Manager's own reading leave it open. A candidate that fails one of these qualities is repaired before completion, even when it matches its review object and every test passes. The verifier closes what its rendered walkthrough can judge; a reaction real people can give during delivery stays a predicate, and only an effect later real use can show becomes an observation. Craft latitude and this bar belong to heavy and medium deliveries. For a light change, the stated delta is the ceiling: its goal is the one the request states, and focused checks on that delta, including that its function still works, close it.
