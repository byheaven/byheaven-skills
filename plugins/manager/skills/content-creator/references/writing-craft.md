# Writing Craft

Read before Stage 4. This file is the single authoritative voice definition for all content with the user as the assumed author. Apply it to every draft and carry the Chinese rules verbatim into Chinese drafting directives.

## Voice Target

The target register is Paul Graham: reason from first principles; decisive and direct, unafraid of strong claims; contrarian by default — challenge the popular narrative, see through surface consensus.

Chinese tone: reduce preaching. Less second-person admonition and rhetorical nudging; more first-person judgment stated with evidence.

## Author × Reader

Before drafting, declare the author and a concrete reader. Name the human when ghostwriting. Specify what the reader already knows, cares about, and would accept as evidence; platform labels alone are too broad.

For agent-authored work, write at the reader's altitude. For content ghostwritten as Yu Bai, write in the Voice Target defined above — the relevant facet of his voice, not an agent's explainer register.

## Long-form Principles

- Advance one sharp explanation model. Lead with the observation or mechanism that gives the piece its force.
- Every sentence adds evidence, names a mechanism, shows a consequence, exposes a cost, or sharpens the reader's lens.
- Write for what the named reader knows. Cut previews, throat-clearing, repeated conclusions, and claims without evidence they would accept.
- Compression demonstrates understanding: the second draft is shorter than the first unless added evidence justifies growth.
- Stop when the argument earns its final sentence.

## Public Voice Gate

Run this after correctness is settled and before delivery. Add no new ideas.

- The piece advances one sharp explanation model rather than surveying a topic.
- Each paragraph moves that model forward.
- Strong claims stay calm. Frame unverified motives as explanatory models, not facts.
- Memorable lines compress the reasoning into a portable lens; never manufacture aphorisms. A memorable line is short, hard, concrete, slightly counterintuitive; it stops when the compression lands and never explains itself.
- Compress, reorder, cut scaffolding, and replace explainer voice with judgment.
- The ending changes how the reader sees the subject. Rewrite a draft that still reads like an agent explaining Yu Bai's idea.

## Chinese Hard Rules

1. 不用「不是X，是Y」及其变体（「不是X而是Y」「并非X而是Y」）。正当的对比论证不受限，如「X固然重要，但Y才是关键」。
2. 破折号克制使用。可以用在关键停顿处，不能当填充符号或偷懒替代句子结构。
3. 中文正文不夹英文。英文只保留真专名和没有确立中译的术语。可译词、框架、铺垫和旁白均使用中文。
4. 不用比喻（含明喻、暗喻）。用字面陈述代替形象类比；机制本身立得住就不需要比喻背书。

禁用空话和套话，如「众所周知」「值得注意的是」「简单来说」「我个人认为」「不可否认」「小编认为」「亲测有效」「建议收藏」。Post-draft verification may use any reliable text-search surface to find `不是.*是` candidates for manual review. Use Obsidian CLI when a check depends on index, properties, tags, backlinks, or plugin semantics.

## English Long-form Rules

Use short declarative sentences. Keep one idea per sentence. Prefer plain words and active voice. Use one qualifier for an uncertain claim, not a stack of hedges. Remove filler frames, redundant connectives, decorative adverbs, and AI sign-offs. Conceive the piece in English from the shared argument skeleton; never translate the Chinese draft.
