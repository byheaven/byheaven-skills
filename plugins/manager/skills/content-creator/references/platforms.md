# Platform Limits

Hard limits each platform enforces. Length, structure, and tone within them come from the writing brief.

- **X**: 280 characters per post; a longer piece is a numbered thread.
- **Xiaohongshu**: title up to 20 characters; body up to 1,000 Chinese characters; hashtags written as `#标签名`.
- **Blog**: the canonical long-form version of a piece; same-language adaptations such as Xiaohongshu and Telegram posts are compressed from it.
