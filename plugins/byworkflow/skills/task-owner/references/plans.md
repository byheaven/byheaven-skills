# Plans

## Before a plan settles

A product or technical plan whose choice is consequential or costly to reverse settles on observation rather than on review of the abstract plan. Compare viable routes that differ in mechanism, inside the owner's constraints and including the current approach while it is still admissible; before choosing, test within current authority each assumption that would change the route, and bind the choice to the result. An assumption still untested keeps the commitments that depend on it provisional, and the plan says which; open questions in the plan are answered the same way. For a plan costly to reverse, whoever settles it carries one question into its review directive: how could this plan meet its written Acceptance and still fail its purpose? The reviewer returns each supported failure mechanism as a finding and each examined one it rejects with the reason.
