# Production topology and integration

One task owner keeps the locked Intent and Acceptance and owns integration continuously from the start of Delivery through completion.

Producer and judge work to the same standards, whoever produces the change. The `code-production` skill holds this plugin's code standards and methods, and the `experience-design` skill those for anything a person experiences in a product; loading either is the producer's or judge's choice, and imports methods, not the sub-agent's ownership or lifecycle boundaries. Repository commit and integration follow [repo-integration.md](repo-integration.md).

## Topology

The task owner chooses self-production or a bounded dispatch under P3's hand-off reasons in the deployment's always-loaded rules. Accountability stays with the owner: another writer is an execution choice, not a transfer of the Goal, Acceptance, or integration. A worker charter is optional when a focused directive and the applicable method skills supply the whole slice. [Design rounds](design-rounds.md) holds methods for a taste- or design-load-bearing commitment.

Concurrent writers need locked shared authority and interfaces, bounded Acceptance slices, and disjoint write surfaces, including any judge that modifies an artifact to test it. Unresolved interfaces, overlapping writes, and dependent slices stay serial. Dispatch only with a result route that resumes the owner; end the turn after any remaining independent work instead of waiting in-turn. A sub-agent creates no task state and integrates no peers. It may start sub-agents of its own when its slice gains from them; they inherit its write boundary, its question route and, under a verifier, its read-only independence, and it answers for their results.

**Directive.** Every directive opens with the result its slice must deliver, why that result matters and whom it serves, and who judges it and how; a bare instruction to load a skill is not a result. It then gives what the receiver cannot find for itself: the larger task; the locked objective; its Acceptance slice, stated as behaviors to judge, not as tests to write, or the concrete question; the user's settled decisions, quoted with their source; relevant authorities, interfaces, and current facts; the exact write boundary (for repository work, the worktree and its host-created or named branch); and where the result goes. A constraint enters a directive only when it carries the user's decision, quoted with its source, or guards a concrete risk, which the directive names; method, tools, format, length, depth, structure, the skills to use and the shape of the result stay with the receiver. The directive asks the receiver to trust its ability and deliver a result beyond what the user expects. A fresh sub-agent inherits neither the task owner's reads nor linked files, so the directive carries what the sub-agent needs from a task-owner reference, such as [Governed changes](governed-changes.md). **Global-rules excerpt.** Any directive, packet, or hand-over sent to a session that does not load the user's global rules (a producer, investigator, verifier, derivation route, or one-shot CLI run) carries what it needs from them in the rules' own words, as the deployment's generated excerpt file, word for word, where one exists: P1–P3 and the applicable Task contract and Memory and Rules duties from the deployment's always-loaded rules, reply language, every applicable floor, and that questions return to its dispatcher, never to the user. For a user-visible surface, the directive carries the approved review object itself or a reference to it, never the task owner's paraphrase; the invariants of the [Product decision gate](experience-gate.md#product-decision-gate) govern what implementation adds to it. The parent transcript and peer work stay outside the directive. Raw payloads stay at stable receipt locations the directive names.

**Runtime.** A same-runtime sub-agent receives the runtime-native directive, uses that runtime's agent configuration, and reports through the native result channel. The model and reasoning effort every producer, verifier, and derivation route runs, and the fallback when one is unavailable or out of quota, come from the deployment's model routing when the user's instructions name one; the runtime's configuration supplies only the invocation. For a shape commitment Direction left open (a module boundary or interface others depend on, a stored-data shape, or anything costly to reverse), bring in a producer from another model family for a [competing candidate](design-rounds.md#competing-candidates) only when it would likely change the choice. Cross-runtime or cross-model production needs a P3 hand-off reason; a high-risk predicate's judge is cross-model under [Owner acceptance](evidence.md#owner-acceptance). Justified cross-runtime production uses its configured carrier; review follows [Verification carrier](runtimes.md#verification-carrier).

## Re-evaluation

On every material result or authority or interface change, re-evaluate each pending and active sub-agent against its locked premise, Acceptance binding, and expected remaining value. A pending one that remains valid stays eligible; an active one that remains valid and valuable receives only the newly relevant facts. When the premise becomes invalid or remaining value no longer justifies the work, skip dispatching it, stop it, and reject its result from integration. A foundational Direction, shared-authority, or interface change applies the same rule to every affected sub-agent; dispatch a fresh bounded slice from the new authority only when work remains valuable. An unaffected sub-agent continues only when the update provably leaves its assumptions, write scope, and Acceptance binding intact; followups that preserve invalid context are not continuation.

## Integration

The task owner integrates every topology into one candidate. At a join, start from distilled findings and stable references to raw receipts, and open the exact evidence integration or judgment calls for. Files, batches, plan steps and drafts are not deliverables. Merge adjacent outputs before the next commitment boundary. During production, run targeted checks mapped to changed predicates. When artifact state and write permissions permit, independent deterministic closure such as consumer audit, architecture-document reachability, projection freshness, runtime smoke, and test shards may run concurrently and join once. Broaden to full regression only when the actual impact surface or dependency uncertainty requires it. Where the workspace maintains a behavior-assertion registry for its user-visible surfaces, which of its executable carriers run before owner acceptance, end-to-end included, is the [`qa`](../../qa/SKILL.md) method's call; a red carrier is repaired in-task through the repair-now route. Capture an independent candidate-state list only when drift risk across repositories, runtimes, artifacts, concurrent writers, or a long wait makes the observed state otherwise ambiguous. Neither is a fixed closeout step.

Evidence is assigned, preserved, and reminted per predicate under [evidence.md](evidence.md). When independent semantic judgment is warranted, identify one locked commitment identity and exact state, then select one verifier or a parallel wave divided by real specialist capability, observation method, or model blind spot. Join the one review round, then settle its findings under [Verifier dispatch](evidence.md#verifier-dispatch). Rework on a dispatched writer slice resumes that writer's session (its worktree for code) with only the findings to fix; only when resuming is unsupported or fails does a fresh run get the original directive and the open findings. Resolved parts are not replayed without invalidation. A materially new commitment gets a fresh verifier-need assessment. Unknown remains open until settled, judged unjudgeable under [Predicate closure](evidence.md#predicate-closure), or returned to Direction when its bounds change.
