# Design rounds

## Competing candidates

For a taste- or design-load-bearing commitment, N independent candidates for the same target surface may be drafted concurrently, each on its own disjoint scratch surface without shared drafts, followed by exactly one selection. Candidates never co-write the shared target; only the selected candidate is integrated. Use this when output variance is high and the predicate's judge can rank real samples; mechanical work with one correct shape stays single-candidate. When user taste owns the standard, the user picks among real samples delivered asynchronously while the task owner continues selection-independent work. When the standard is objectifiable against a named authority, fix scorable criteria from it and Acceptance before fan-out; The task owner scores every candidate against them; add an independent scorer from another model family only when it would likely change the pick, and resolve any disagreement before picking. When they diverge so widely that the scorers cannot settle on a pick, the task is unclear: reframe it rather than averaging the candidates. The scores and rationale are kept with the delivered state. The selected candidate, optionally grafting cleanly separable strengths from losers, enters the ordinary integration and evidence path. Losing candidates leave the integration path; retain only useful comparative evidence in the project's research or learning record, if any.

## Polishing loop

A heavy or medium design is polished in rounds once a candidate is chosen; a plan is not; probes answer its open questions under [Before a plan settles](plans.md#before-a-plan-settles). Before the first round, fix the benchmark, the qualities from Acceptance, and how they are compared, and hold them across rounds unless Direction changes. Each round compares the real build, observed directly by an observer without the producer's reasoning (a [walkthrough](../../qa/references/walkthrough.md) is one way), against the benchmark, counting needless length and internal contradictions as gaps; the producer closes those gaps and cuts what no longer earns its place. A claimed gain cites the change made and its observed effect; scores only summarize that comparison. Polishing stops when every quality is at or above the benchmark or a round brings no observed gain. Stopping ends polishing only: an unmet Acceptance quality stays open, a defect goes through [Failure diagnosis](evidence.md#failure-diagnosis), and a refuted premise reopens the route. Each round's comparison, gaps, and changes stay with the delivery. The final state follows [Owner acceptance](evidence.md#owner-acceptance); any assigned judge derives challenge cases from Intent and raw evidence, not loop ratings.
