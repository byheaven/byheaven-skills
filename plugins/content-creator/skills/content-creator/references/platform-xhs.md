# Xiaohongshu (RED) Guidelines

## Format Constraints

- Title: ≤ 20 characters, accurate and substantive — no curiosity bait, no emoji padding
- Body: 150-300 characters for standard posts; Xiaohongshu article-style posts must stay under 1000 Chinese characters unless the caller gives a stricter limit
- End with 2-3 hashtags (format: `#标签名`), no more than 3
- Structure: hook → argument context → key points → takeaway
- Use line breaks and short paragraphs for visual rhythm — walls of text perform poorly on Xiaohongshu
- Emoji may be used sparingly for visual rhythm (not banned, not required)

## Voice and Tone

Xiaohongshu uses a professional, human voice. Keep the argument clear, specific, and judgment-led; adapt for feed reading through structure, pacing, and concrete examples, not by downshifting into a casual or chatty tone. The hook must be faithful to the source material — never oversimplify, exaggerate, or manufacture curiosity to game engagement.

Do not stack emoji. Do not use exclamation marks as energy substitutes. Do not write like a marketing account. Do not add casual filler, influencer slang, or forced intimacy to sound "more human."

## Adaptation from Canonical Essay

Xiaohongshu posts are **same-language compressions** of the canonical Chinese long-form essay — editorial re-creation that preserves the argument skeleton while adjusting length, structure, and tone for the platform's feed-reading context. This is compression, not dumbing-down.
